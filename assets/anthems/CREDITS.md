## Custom Jump-up — uploaded DNB

`dnb.mp3` comes from the user-supplied file
`Screen Recording 2026-11-05 125659.mp3`, provided for use as the game's DNB anthem.
The first 0.41 seconds of silence are removed, gain is increased by 5.73 dB to
match the other clips, and short edge fades prevent clicks. The remaining
approximately 16 seconds are retained in stereo, without changing tempo or pitch.

This recording is not generated by the synthesis script and is not covered by
the synthesized-track MIT grant below.

## High Rise Solitude — uploaded rap

`rap.mp3` is an 18.462-second goal edit of the user-supplied AI track
`High_Rise_Solitude.mp3` (original duration approximately 150.94 seconds).
It uses source time **0.064–18.526 seconds**, starting near the opening beat.

Both new edits retain stereo, tempo and pitch. They use an 8 ms fade-in and
180 ms edge fade-out to prevent clicks, with two-pass loudness normalization
to approximately -16 LUFS and a -1.8 dBTP ceiling, followed by 44.1 kHz stereo
MP3 encoding. In-game replay-ending fades still run through the remaining clip.
The uploaded tracks were provided for use in Rocket Ball; they are not covered
by the synthesized-demo MIT grant below.

## Original synthesized demos

- **East Stand** — grime instrumental, 140 BPM.
- **Touchline** — rap/hip-hop instrumental, 92 BPM.

These two former goal clips were composed for Rocket Ball with
`tools/compose-goal-anthems.py`, using synthesized drums, bass and melodic parts.
No sampled songs, artists' recordings, Spotify audio or external audio were used
for these synthesized clips. They are distributed under the project's MIT license.

Render the original demos using Python, numpy, scipy and ffmpeg. The script
writes `grime-demo.mp3` and `rap-demo.mp3`, preserving the uploaded goal
songs. Its optional `--track dnb` renders the former synthetic **Afterburn**
jump-up demo to `afterburn-demo.mp3` instead.
