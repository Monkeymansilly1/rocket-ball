# Original goal anthems

These original instrumental clips were composed for Rocket Ball with
`tools/compose-goal-anthems.py`, using synthesized drums, bass and melodic parts.
No sampled songs, artists' recordings, Spotify audio or external audio were used.
They are distributed under the project's MIT license.

- **Afterburn** — drum and bass, 172 BPM.
- **East Stand** — grime instrumental, 140 BPM.
- **Touchline** — rap/hip-hop instrumental, 92 BPM.

MP3 assets are normalized for consistent playback, with stereo mixes and a
limited true peak. Recreate them using Python, numpy, scipy and ffmpeg.
