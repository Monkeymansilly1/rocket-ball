Keyboard aerial handling

Manual mode: W pitches nose down, S pitches nose up in the air. Boost follows your car's nose, so keep it aimed up to climb. Assisted mode: hold Space to jump, then hold Shift to raise the nose and climb; W is optional. K raises the nose and I lowers it in both modes. Boost alone does not lift a grounded car.
